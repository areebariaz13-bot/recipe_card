1. Classic Chocolate Brownies

These classic chocolate brownies are soft, rich and fudgy. They are perfect for chocolate lovers!

Preparation Time: 15 minutes
Baking Time: 25 minutes
Serving: 4 people

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup melted chocolate
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup melted butter
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla essence

Preparation Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix melted butter and sugar in a bowl.
  3. Add eggs and vanilla essence.
  4. Mix in the melted chocolate and flour.
  5. Pour the mixture into a greased baking tray.
  6. Bake for 25 minutes and allow to cool.

2. Chocolate Walnut Brownies

These brownies combine delicious chocolate with crunchy walnuts. They are a great choice for anyone who enjoys a little crunch in every bite.

Preparation Time: 15 minutes
Baking Time: 25–30 minutes
Serving: 4 people

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup melted butter
  • 1/2 cup chopped walnuts
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla essence

Preparation Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix melted butter and sugar together.
  3. Add eggs and vanilla essence.
  4. Add flour and cocoa powder and mix well.
  5. Fold in the chopped walnuts.
  6. Pour the mixture into a baking tray.
  7. Bake for 25–30 minutes and let them cool.

3. Double Chocolate Brownies

If you love extra chocolate, this recipe is for you! These brownies are filled with chocolate chunks for an extra rich and delicious taste.

Preparation Time: 15 minutes
Baking Time: 25 minutes
Serving: 4 people

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup melted butter
  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla essence

Preparation Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix butter and sugar in a large bowl.
  3. Add eggs and vanilla essence.
  4. Add flour and cocoa powder.
  5. Mix in the chocolate chips.
  6. Pour the batter into a greased baking tray.
  7. Bake for 25 minutes and allow to cool.
