Brownie Baking Tips

  • Measure carefully: Use the correct amount of every ingredient.
  • Do not overmix: Mix just until the ingredients are combined.
  • Preheat the oven: Let it reach 180°C before baking.
  • Check the centre: A toothpick with a few moist crumbs is suitable for fudgy brownies.
  • Let them cool: Cool brownies before cutting neat pieces.

Common Baking Mistakes

Brownies are too dry

They may have baked for too long. Check them before the full baking time is over.

Brownies are too soft

They may need a few more minutes in the oven. Let them cool fully before cutting.

Brownies are difficult to cut

Allow them to cool and use a clean knife.
