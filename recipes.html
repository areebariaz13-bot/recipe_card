Choose a recipe and enjoy baking delicious brownies!

1. Classic Chocolate Brownies

Preparation: 15 minutes | Baking: 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup melted butter
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla essence

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix butter and sugar.
  3. Add eggs and vanilla.
  4. Stir in flour and cocoa powder.
  5. Bake for about 25 minutes.

2. Chocolate Walnut Brownies

Preparation: 15 minutes | Baking: 25–30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup melted butter
  • 1/2 cup chopped walnuts

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C.
  2. Whisk eggs, sugar and butter.
  3. Add flour and cocoa powder.
  4. Fold in chopped walnuts.
  5. Bake for 25–30 minutes.

3. Double Chocolate Brownies

Preparation: 15 minutes | Baking: 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup melted butter
  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix butter and sugar.
  3. Beat in eggs.
  4. Stir in flour and cocoa powder.
  5. Add chocolate chips and bake for 25 minutes.
